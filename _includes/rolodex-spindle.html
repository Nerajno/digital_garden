{%- comment -%} The Rolodex "spindle" (/note/Rolodex): one term card at a time, built from _data/terms.yml. Every card renders on the server; assets/js/spindle.js shows one at a time and keeps the current card in the URL hash. Without JS the [data-js-only] controls stay hidden and every card shows as an A–Z list. Only letters with terms get a tab; on narrow screens the tabs move below the card (garden.css 08b).
